Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Borno Data ("we," "our," "the app") provides mobile data, airtime, and related VTU (Virtual Top-Up) services in Nigeria. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how we protect it.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: full name, email address, and phone number when you register.
  • Transaction information: records of data, airtime, and other purchases you make, including amounts, recipient phone numbers, and timestamps.
  • Wallet and payment information: your wallet balance, funding history, and the dedicated virtual bank account number generated for you by our payment partner (Billstack) to fund your wallet. We do not store your bank card details or banking login credentials — funding is handled directly through bank transfer to your dedicated account.
  • Device information: basic technical information such as IP address and device type, used for security and fraud prevention.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and wallet
  • To process your data, airtime, and other purchases through our service providers
  • To detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized account access
  • To provide customer support
  • To send you service-related notifications (e.g. transaction confirmations, site announcements)

How We Protect Your Information

Your password and transaction PIN are never stored in readable form — both are one-way hashed, meaning even we cannot see or recover them, only reset them if you lose access. Sensitive requests to our servers are encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Third Parties We Work With

To deliver our services, we share the minimum necessary information with:

  • Billstack — for wallet funding via dedicated virtual bank accounts
  • Our data/airtime delivery partners — to fulfill your data and airtime purchases (only your phone number and the plan purchased are shared, as needed to deliver the service)

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or any other third party.

Your Choices

  • You can update your account details at any time within the app.
  • You can request account deletion by contacting support — this permanently removes your account, wallet, and transaction history.
  • You can contact us with any privacy questions using the details below.

Children's Privacy

Borno Data is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at support@bornodata.com or via WhatsApp support in the app.
